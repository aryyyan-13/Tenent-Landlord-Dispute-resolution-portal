MUNICIPAL CODE § 8.4(b) Enacted Oct 2021 • Amended Jun 2024

Mandatory Security Deposit Restitution, Interest Computations, and Treble Damages Timelines

Jurisdiction: Ward 4 Residential Leases • Standard Penalty Provision: Treble Default
verified Certified Legislative Text Vol. 44, Sec. 18-b

“(1) Upon tenant surrender of the premises and return of all keys or access devices, the lessor shall have a strict statutory window of precisely thirty (30) calendar days to transmit either (A) the entire escrowed security deposit sum with statutory municipal interest accrued, or (B) a verified itemized ledger enumerating reasonable deductions for actual damages beyond standard wear and tear, accompanied by exact receipts and unexpended residual balances.”

“(2) Failure of the lessor to remit the full security deposit or itemized notification within forty-five (45) calendar days from receipt of written demand by registered mail or verified TLDRP civic portal filing constitutes prima facie evidence of bad faith. Upon such finding by the neutral hearing officer, the lessor shall be mandatorily assessed statutory treble damages (300% of the withheld amount), plus reasonable mediation administration costs and statutory interest computed at 5.25% per annum.”

psychology_alt Plain-Language Translation: What This Means for You
person For Tenants
  • You must receive your deposit back or a detailed list of deductions within 30 calendar days of turning in keys.
  • Landlords cannot deduct for normal wear and tear (such as faded paint or minor scuffs).
  • If 45 days pass without restitution or legitimate deductions, you qualify to file a TLDRP claim for 3x the withheld amount.
real_estate_agent For Property Owners / Landlords
  • Deductions require itemized receipts or contractor estimates attached directly to the move-out ledger.
  • All escrow funds must have accrued the municipal minimum interest rate (currently 2.1% base).
  • Missing the 30-day notice window completely invalidates right to deduct from escrowed deposit amounts.
